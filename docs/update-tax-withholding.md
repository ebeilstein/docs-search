Change how much federal or state tax is taken out of each paycheck. *(Sample content: replace with your real procedure.)*

## Before you start
- The IRS **Tax Withholding Estimator** can help you decide what to enter.

## Steps
1. Sign in to the **employee self-service portal**.
2. Go to **Pay** → **Tax Withholding**.
3. Choose **Federal (W-4)** or **State (DE 4)**.
4. Fill out the form. Each section has help text if you're unsure.
5. Sign electronically and click **Submit**.
   The change applies to the next payroll that hasn't been processed yet.

## If something goes wrong
- **Not sure what to enter:** talk to a tax professional. Payroll staff can't give tax advice.
