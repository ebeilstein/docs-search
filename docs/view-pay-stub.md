Find, view, and save your pay statements. *(Sample content: replace with your real procedure.)*

## Steps
1. Sign in to the **employee self-service portal**.
2. Go to **Pay** → **Pay Statements**.
3. Pick the **pay date** you want from the list.
4. Click **View** to see it, or **Download PDF** to save or print it.

## If something goes wrong
- **Pay date not listed:** statements appear on the morning of payday.
- **A number looks wrong:** contact Payroll before the next pay period.
