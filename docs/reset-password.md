Use this guide if you forgot your password or your account is locked. *(Sample content: replace with your real procedure.)*

## Before you start
- You need access to the email address or phone number on file.
- If your account is locked after too many attempts, wait 15 minutes before trying again.

## Steps
1. Open the **employee sign-in page** in your web browser.
2. Under the password box, click **Forgot password?**
3. Enter your **employee ID or work email**, then click **Send reset link**.
   If you don't see the email within 5 minutes, check your junk/spam folder.
4. Open the email and click **Reset my password**. The link expires after 30 minutes.
5. Choose a new password. It must be at least 12 characters and different from your last 5 passwords.
6. Click **Save**, then sign in with your new password.

## If something goes wrong
- **No email arrived:** confirm the email on file with IT, then try again.
- **Link expired:** go back to step 2 and request a new link.
- **Still locked out:** contact the IT Help Desk.
