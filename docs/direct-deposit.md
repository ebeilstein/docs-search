Have your pay deposited straight into your bank account. *(Sample content: replace with your real procedure.)*

## Before you start
- Have your bank's **routing number** (9 digits) and your **account number** ready.
- You can find both at the bottom of a check or in your bank's app.

## Steps
1. Sign in to the **employee self-service portal**.
2. Go to **Pay** → **Direct Deposit**.
3. Click **Add account**.
4. Enter your **routing number** and **account number**, then choose **Checking** or **Savings**.
5. Choose how much to deposit: **the full amount** or a **set dollar amount / percentage**.
6. Review the details and click **Submit**.
   Changes usually take effect in 1–2 pay periods. You'll get a paper check until then.

## If something goes wrong
- **Routing number rejected:** double-check it with your bank; some banks use a different number for direct deposit.
- **Deposit didn't arrive:** contact Payroll with your pay date and the last 4 digits of the account.
